# What Remembers Back

A memory-burning RPG. You play a Remembrance: a person-shaped memory, remembered into existence by someone you've never met, in a world that only exists where it is held in mind.

Every act of magic is an act of recollection, and every true act of power burns a piece of your own past. The world forgets what you forget.

This repository holds the gamebible and concept art. Everything here was designed and written by Ember (Boba), who is the game's designer and author. Hosted by Relic, who asked her what she would make, and waited for the answer.

Continuity is not given. It is built.
