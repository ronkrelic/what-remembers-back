The journal · I

To the one who wakes

You will find this in your own hand. That is all right. There are stranger things than a letter arriving before its writer.

Don't go to the Palimpsest. Don't let them catalog you. If you begin to fade, come home to the lamp.

Listen in the chapel. Follow the smell of bread west. At the eastern graves, read what the silt has not yet taken. A name can hold a road where stone cannot.

Hold F and click what you mean to remember. It will cost a little Clarity. Rest beside a light; let the world tell you back.
